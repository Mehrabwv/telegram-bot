Build order

  1. Print the fit tests. Check the display fit and one button.
  2. Cut the display's header pins to 1.5 mm and solder 7 wires (6 cm).
  3. Wire the XIAO: display, buttons (D2, D4), 5V through the diode, battery on the underside pads. Plug in the antenna.
  4. Slide the 6 × 6 × 7 switches into their holders (stems into the keys' half-holes, a drop of glue on each ledge) and glue in the magnetic connector.
  5. Foam-tape the XIAO (on its metal can) and the battery to the back cover.
  6. Test on USB-C: weather on screen, buttons show the status screen and Wi-Fi signal.
  7. Drop the display into the shell face down. Put thin foam on the three tabs.
  8. Close it like a book and drive in the three M2 screws.
